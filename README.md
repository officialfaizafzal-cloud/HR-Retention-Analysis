 HR-Retention-Analysis
Project Title: HR Employee Retention & Attrition Analysis

Project Description:

Analyzed employee data to identify key factors influencing employee attrition and retention using SQL, Microsoft Excel, and Power BI.

**Key Responsibilities & Contributions:**

* Cleaned, transformed, and analyzed HR datasets to identify employee attrition patterns.
* Used SQL queries, aggregations, filtering, and joins to extract meaningful workforce insights.
* Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to track employee attrition rate, department-wise attrition, salary distribution, and employee demographics.
* Created Excel dashboards and PivotTables to summarize HR metrics and visualize workforce trends.
* Analyzed attrition by department, age group, job role, salary, and years of service.
* Identified employee segments with higher attrition rates to support data-driven retention strategies.
* Presented key performance indicators (KPIs) and insights to support HR reporting and workforce planning.

Tools & Technologies: SQL, Microsoft Excel, Power BI, DAX, Data Cleaning, Data Visualization, HR Analytics, KPI Reporting.

Project Outcome: Developed a data-driven view of employee attrition patterns and highlighted areas where HR teams could investigate opportunities to improve employee retention.
